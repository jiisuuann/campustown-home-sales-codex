READING THE DATA

A clear view starts
with clear assumptions.

Every sale counts. Prices have a floor.

Sales totals include every unique listing ID. Price summaries exclude missing prices and sales below $1,000. Higher prices are retained, including reported outliers; the final chart band includes all sales of $2 million or more.

A snapshot, not a market forecast.

Values are as reported by the collection source, not verified against county records. Missing values are never treated as zero. Addresses are not used to deduplicate sales. Property type is unreliable and is not used here.
